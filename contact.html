Quick FAQs

Common questions about our equipment rental service

1 How far in advance should I book equipment?
We recommend booking at least 2-4 weeks in advance for standard events, and 2-3 months for large-scale events like weddings or corporate galas. This ensures equipment availability and gives us time to plan your setup in detail.
2 Do you deliver and set up the equipment?
Yes! All of our rental packages include delivery, professional setup, and pickup after your event. Our team arrives early to ensure everything is tested and ready before your guests arrive.
3 What happens if equipment breaks during my event?
All our equipment is fully tested before delivery and backed by our reliability guarantee. In the unlikely event of a technical issue, our 24/7 support line connects you with a technician who can resolve most problems remotely or dispatch a replacement immediately.
4 Can I customize a package for my specific event?
Absolutely! We offer fully customizable packages tailored to your event type, venue, guest count, and budget. Contact us with your requirements and our team will put together a personalized quote for you.
5 What areas do you serve?
We primarily serve the Jakarta metropolitan area and surrounding regions. For events outside our standard service area, please contact us and we will arrange logistics on a case-by-case basis.
